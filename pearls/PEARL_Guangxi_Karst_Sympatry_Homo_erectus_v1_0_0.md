Section 2: Triadic Ontological Mapping (Subject - Object - Action)
Subject (-1 Blue / der / Calibration & Archaeometric Frame)
Five independent geochronological clocks—uranium-series disequilibrium on speleothem flowstones, combined electron spin resonance / U-series (ESR/U-series) on dental enamel, and optically stimulated luminescence (OSL) on cave sediments—anchoring an irreversible 200,000-year mainland persistence extension for Homo erectus (shifting regional terminal estimates from approximately 400 ka down to 184 ka).
Object (0 Green / die / Medium & Karst Boundary Layer)
Subtropical closed-canopy karst rainforest biome in Guangxi functioning as a stable thermodynamic refugium. Characterized by C3-dominated canopy photosynthesis, buffered hydrological sinkholes, and calcitic cave mineralization that preserves dental enamel proteomes and biogenic isotopic signals across deep-time climate cycles.
Action (+1 Red / das / State Transition & Lineage Sympatry)
Multi-lineage sympatric overlap in mainland East Asia: late-surviving East Asian Homo erectus coexisting alongside Denisovan / Homo longi populations prior to late-Pleistocene Homo sapiens dispersals, conclusively falsifying the Savanna-Only Hominin Niche Hypothesis.

Section 3: Multi-Proxy Archaeometric & Molecular Audit
Geochronological Triangulation
Cross-verification between direct enamel ESR/U-series dates and bounding speleothem calcite ages in Baxian and Naxian karst chambers eliminates stratigraphic reworking or taphonomic transport artifacts. By sandwiching hominin fossil layers between flowstone horizons dated via high-precision thermal ionization mass spectrometry (TIMS) U-series, the chronostratigraphic framework provides an unassailable minimum and maximum terminus for both taxa.
Enamel Paleoproteomics
Dental enamel acts as a closed mineral vault (96% hydroxyapatite) protecting peptide sequences far beyond the ~400-800 ka tropical ancient DNA decay horizon. Single amino acid polymorphisms (SAPs) retrieved via high-resolution tandem mass spectrometry resolve distinct lineage markers across four key structural proteins:


* Amelogenin (AMELX/AMELY): Establishes biological sex and provides foundational sequence divergence metrics separating archaic hominin clades.
* Enamelin (ENAM): Contains target SAPs that resolve a distinct mainland East Asian Homo erectus clade separate from island Southeast Asian (Ngandong/Trinil) populations.
* MMP20 (Enamelysin): Conserved cleavage site variants confirm structural preservation and rule out post-mortem exogenous protein degradation.
* COL17A1 (Collagen Type XVII Alpha 1): Unambiguously distinguishes the ninth molar as belonging to the Denisovan / Homo longi lineage, separate from the surrounding eight Homo erectus specimens.
Biogenic Stable & Radiogenic Isotope Vectors
* Carbon-13 ($\delta^{13}\text{C}$): Strongly depleted values (-14‰ to -16‰) are diagnostic of a closed-canopy C3 forest floor and arboreal resources ("canopy effect"), ruling out open C4 savanna foraging and proving adaptation to dense rainforest interiors.
* Oxygen-18 ($\delta^{18}\text{O}$): High meteoric/leaf-water enrichment reflects humid monsoonal canopy transpiration regimes and local microclimate hydrologic cycles within the karst basin.
* Strontium-87/86 ($^{87}\text{Sr}/^{87}\text{Sr}$): Spatial variance measured across enamel growth increments tracks deliberate, structured landscape mobility between distinct geological terranes (carbonate karst towers vs. surrounding silicate uplands).

Section 4: Compendium & Theoretical Integration
Karst Refugium Isomorphism
This system links Guangxi tower-karst cave systems directly to the Mississippian karst refugium model in the Dandelion Compendium. It demonstrates how subterranean limestone matrices preserve high-fidelity biological and physical state vectors across deep time. The microclimatic buffering of deep karst chambers shields ambient biological material from macroclimatic thermal swings, creating localized micro-environments where archaic lineages persist through glacial-interglacial transitions.
Falsification of Single-Niche Linear Replacement
The Guangxi empirical signal replaces the classical linear extinction cascade (Homo erectus vanishes at ~400 ka, replaced sequentially by archaic and then modern lineages) with a braided, multi-lineage sympatric lattice in East Asia lasting until at least 184 ka.

Section 5: ScholarEvolve Verification & Reproducibility Checklist
Gate 1: Stratigraphic Lock
Verify that bounding flowstone U-series ages bracket direct enamel U-series/ESR ages without age inversion across all sampled sub-chambers in Baxian and Naxian caves.
Gate 2: Proteomic FDR & Deamidation
Confirm that liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) peptide spectral matches pass a False Discovery Rate (FDR) threshold of $\le 1%$. Confirm authentic glutamine and asparagine deamidation profiles consistent with ancient peptides, ruling out modern keratin or collagen contamination.
Gate 3: Isotopic Canopy Baseline
Cross-check hominin $\delta^{13}\text{C}$ values against contemporaneous obligate C3 forest fauna (e.g., orangutan Pongo, tapir Tapirus) and open-habitat browsers from the same Baxian/Naxian stratigraphic horizons to validate the closed-canopy baseline.
Gate 4: Sympatric Niche Partitioning
Test $\delta^{13}\text{C}$, $\delta^{18}\text{O}$, and $^{87}\text{Sr}/^{86}\text{Sr}$ separation between the eight Homo erectus teeth and the single Denisovan / Homo longi molar to quantitatively evaluate resource partitioning versus direct ecological niche overlap.
